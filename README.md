It is for the community specifically in barangays, to assist the people about their health and provide a better and faster way to check their health and help them with their needs.	This system serves as a platform lists of the records of all the patients that goes into the health center, this hopefully will help the health center personnel in their work on safekeeping the records of each patients that they serve.

First, we need a patient and the admin who logs in using a registered account, the admin will ask about the important information about the patient and the admin will record that information to this program. This is just the beginning of our developed basic program, we will continue to update this as we also develop our skills as an I.T.

As we make this program, we were really enjoying specially in designing the User Interface, our creativity pumps in our minds, and also how could we avoid some bugs, we have to reach our limit, researching and learning about the bugs we’ve fixed so that we can fix it in the future without eating too much time, the icons and UI were created by our own idea, using some little references through our experience browsing a lot of User Interfaces in the past

About the company and Logo, we were inspired by one of our favorite game, the company called Umbrella Corp. but I tuned it a bit to make it more health friendly to our vision, that’s why we picked the greenish color. 

The sole purpose of this system is simple, it aims to ease the hassle that almost every health center in every place within this country are undergoing, and what we mean is to look at the state of almost every health center in every barangay, some of which are doing records of every patients manually thus making them to exert more effort in such a simple chore and leaving other works with a little time, so to give aid to all of the Health Center workers we decided to make a system that could help them even in such a small way.
